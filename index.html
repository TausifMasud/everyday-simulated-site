Everyday Simulated Uploader

The channel uses a small internal tool, "Everyday Simulated Uploader", to publish its own videos to its own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API: it uploads the rendered video file, sets the title, description, tags, thumbnail and captions, and schedules the release. It is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to other users.
